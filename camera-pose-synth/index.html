Body parameters

Two voices: the left arm plays a sawtooth, the right arm a square wave. Shoulder angle sets the pitch: 0° hanging down, 90° in a T-pose (the base note), 180° straight up. The elbow sets the duty cycle: a straight arm gives a pure saw and a 50% square; bending it skews the saw toward a triangle and narrows the square to a thin pulse.

Angles come from MediaPipe's 3D landmarks. M toggles sound. Everything runs in the browser.

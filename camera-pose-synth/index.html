Body parameters

Two voices: the left arm plays a sawtooth, the right arm a square wave. Shoulder angle sets the pitch: 0° hanging down, 90° in a T-pose (the base note), 180° straight up. The elbow sets the duty cycle from 0% (fully bent) to 100% (straight). Saw: 0% is a falling ramp, 50% a triangle, 100% a rising ramp. Square: 50% is a true square; toward 0% and 100% the pulse gets thin and quiet.

Angles come from MediaPipe's 3D landmarks. M toggles sound. Everything runs in the browser.

## Standalone experiment pages
- Each experiment (simulator, game, tool, visualization, demo) is a single self-contained HTML page (WebGL/WebGPU/three.js allowed). Add it to the gallery/index and the README.
- Before committing, verify in the browser. Check for overlapping text, labels or legends, and missing margins. Check both Chromium and Firefox layout where possible.
- Exported images (Save image/PNG) must render from a clean canvas without UI-panel offsets. Check that the subject is centred.
- When an experiment simulates a real phenomenon, default to a faithful, educational simulation. Do not add game-like mechanics (scores, play-as modes) unless asked.

The mannequin

Every pattern piece is drafted from these measurements. Change one and the cut pieces change with it.

Hips, legs and head

The buttocks reshape the hips without changing the hip girth. Leg girths are shares of the standard proportion; trouser legs are redrafted to fit.

Bust

The shape of the bust. The girth round it stays the Chest / bust measurement above; cups, bodices and shirt fronts are redrafted to fit.

Pose

Moves the figure and the garment on it. The cut pieces stay exactly the same: a pattern does not depend on how you stand.

Pose

A dress form on a stand has no arms or legs to pose. Choose Full figure on the Model page to pose it.

Shoes

Pick what the figure wears on its feet. The shoes stay on while you try the clothes; they are only worn here, not cut or sewn.

The marker

A marker is the cutting plan: every piece laid on the cloth with its grain line (the arrow) parallel to the selvedge. The solid outline is the cutting line; the dashed line 1 cm inside is where the needle will run.

    Assembly line

    Each step goes to the machine a factory would use for it. In a real line the pieces are sewn flat at the machine; they are shown on the form here so you can see where each seam ends up.

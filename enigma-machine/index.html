Operator’s notes

  
Paste only the encrypted message body, not radio headers, indicators or discriminant groups. Demo bodies are already prepared. For indicator procedures, decrypt the indicator with its prescribed settings first, then reset the windows to the recovered message key before decrypting the body.

  
Original Enigma preserves only A–Z, not spaces or punctuation. Decrypted historical text remains German telegraph text: X often separates sentences, Q may represent CH, and spelling errors or padding can remain. This page does not automatically translate or reconstruct spacing.

  
Natural-text preparation is a convenience, not a universal historical procedure: German umlauts become AE/OE/UE, ß becomes SS, other Latin accents are folded, digits are spelled in English, and . ! ? become X. Spaces and remaining non-A–Z characters are removed. This preparation is lossy; reversing the cipher recovers prepared letters, not the original formatting.




Historically compatible Enigma I / M3 / M4 mechanics, including double-stepping. Educational use only — Enigma is not secure modern encryption.

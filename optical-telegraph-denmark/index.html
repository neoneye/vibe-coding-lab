Optical telegraphy · Denmark 1801–1862

The Danish optical telegraph

Before electricity carried messages, Denmark sent them by eye: a chain of masts, about ten kilometres apart, each hung with flaps that a crew set to a number while the next station read it through a telescope and set it again. Pick a year, a start and a destination, write a message from the codebook and watch it cross the country. Click any station to go inside.

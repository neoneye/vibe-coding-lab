The trick, step by step
  1. Under the line: the hidden pipe (the cut-open channel in the middle) stands open and empty. You can pour freely.
  2. Over the lip: pass the critical level and liquid arcs over the pipe’s mouth and plunges down the channel.
  3. The plug: the falling column seals the pipe. Air can’t get back in.
  4. The suck: gravity drags the plugged column downward, lowering the pressure inside. Air pressure on the drink’s surface shoves liquid up and over the lip to replace it — the same reason a straw works.
  5. The glug: only when the cup is empty does air finally break the siphon. Not a drop is left for you.

Said to be invented by Pythagoras of Samos (~530 BC) to ration wine: take your fair share, or leave with nothing.

Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction

A thin layer of bromate, malonic acid and a metal catalyst does not settle quietly to equilibrium. Blue waves of oxidised catalyst travel through the red solution, cancel where they meet, and curl into spirals wherever a wave is broken. This page integrates the standard model of that chemistry and lets you poke the dish: touch it with a silver wire, wipe a wave, shine light on it, build walls, change the recipe.
